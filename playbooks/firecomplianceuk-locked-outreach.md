# FireComplianceUK — Locked Outreach Operating Playbook

Status: LOCKED
Purpose: Prevent improvisation during external outreach and preserve approved workflows.

## NON-NEGOTIABLE EXECUTION RULE
Once a workflow or CTA is approved, do not redesign, substitute, simplify, or improvise it during execution. If the approved path cannot be followed, stop that specific action and flag the blocker. Never silently replace it with a manual process.

## Provider workflow
1. Positive provider reply -> thank them and direct them to the FireComplianceUK provider registration/onboarding flow.
2. Do NOT ask positive providers to email onboarding information manually unless Tosin explicitly instructs this for that specific case.
3. Provider company profile, services, geographic coverage, certifications, pricing/rate information and other onboarding data belong in the platform onboarding flow.
4. Provider question -> answer the question accurately, then return to the registration/onboarding CTA where appropriate.
5. Registration -> profile completion -> review/approval -> match-ready.
6. Email correspondence must not become a substitute CRM/onboarding system.

## Facilities manager / client workflow
1. Keep demand-side contacts separate from service providers.
2. Facilities managers and other potential clients receive the approved client/opportunity outreach CTA, never the provider CTA.
3. Current outreach target: at least 500 relevant facilities-manager contacts from the approved database(s).
4. Avoid duplicate outreach and respect previous responses/status.
5. Track contacted, responded, interested, opportunity identified, registered/onboarded and next action.

## Outbound email controls
1. BCC tosin.bankole20@gmail.com on every FireComplianceUK outbound email.
2. Before sending any batch, verify:
   - recipient type
   - previous conversation/history
   - approved template/version
   - correct CTA
   - correct destination/link
   - BCC present
   - no duplicate contact/send
   - personalization does not alter the approved workflow
3. A new campaign/template must be validated before scaling. Once approved/validated, lock the workflow and only personalize permitted fields.
4. Do not change CTA, process, qualification logic or onboarding route without explicit approval.
5. One-off corrections do not become new policy.

## Template locking
Use versioned templates/workflows. Example:
Provider Positive Response v1 — LOCKED
Only permitted personalization: recipient name, company name, relevant response context and other explicitly approved variable fields.
Locked elements include: purpose, CTA, onboarding route and required BCC.

## Failure behaviour
If the registration link, site, sending account, database or another required dependency is unavailable:
STOP the affected send.
Report the exact blocker.
Do not invent an alternative workflow.
Do not ask recipients to perform manual work that the platform was designed to collect unless explicitly authorized.

Contacted -> Responded -> Registration Sent -> Registered -> Review/Approved -> Match Ready

## Client / Facilities Manager status pipeline
Identified -> Qualified -> Contacted -> Responded -> Interested -> Opportunity Identified -> Registered/Onboarded -> Active

## Pre-send gate
No bulk outreach may proceed unless all are TRUE:
- recipient segment is correct
- approved locked template is selected
- CTA matches recipient segment
- destination works
- BCC rule is satisfied
- duplicates/suppressions have been checked
- previous replies have been respected

If any check fails, stop that email or batch rather than improvise.
